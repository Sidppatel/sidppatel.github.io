ABOUT

Where I sit in the stack

I work at the intersection of data engineering and business intelligence - the layer that translates raw multi-source operational data into the metrics, models, and dashboards that drive decisions. I'm not a pure BI developer or a pure pipeline engineer - I'm the person who makes both layers play together so the answer in the dashboard actually means what the executive thinks it means.

My favorite problems are the ones where the data is messy on purpose: legacy ERPs, IoT sensor streams with inconsistent schemas, audits that expect ISO 9001 traceability. The fix is rarely glamorous - it's usually a careful semantic layer, well-named columns, automated validation, and a governance contract everyone actually reads.
